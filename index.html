How to play

There are 3 specimens a day, and everyone gets the same ones. For each one, decide whether it's a real fossil or a phoney. Phoneys don't turn up every day, but watch out: they include rocks that only look like fossils, modern lookalikes, fakes and hoaxes. If you think it's real, guess how long ago it lived.

Each specimen is worth up to 100 points. Spotting a phoney scores 100. For a real fossil, a guess inside its age range scores 100, and the further off you are, the fewer points you get.
