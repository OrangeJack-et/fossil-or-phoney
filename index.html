How to play

There are 3 specimens a day, and everyone gets the same ones. For each one, decide whether it's a real fossil or a phoney. Phoneys include rocks that only look like fossils, modern lookalikes, replicas, fakes and hoaxes. If you think it's real, guess how long ago it lived.
